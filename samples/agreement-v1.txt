FREELANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 1 October 2026 between Northwind Digital Ltd ("the Client") and Alex Morgan ("the Contractor").

1. Definitions
1.1 "Deliverables" means all work product created by the Contractor under this Agreement, including drafts, source files and any materials the Contractor owned before this Agreement that are used in the work.
1.2 "Fees" means the amounts set out in Schedule A.

2. Services
2.1 The Contractor will design and build the Client's customer website as described in the project brief.
2.2 The Client may change the scope of the Services at any time by written notice. The Fees will not change unless the Client agrees otherwise in writing.

3. Payment
3.1 The Client will pay the Fees within ninety (90) days of receiving a valid invoice.
3.2 The Client may withhold any payment if it is not satisfied with the Deliverables.
3.3 Invoices must be submitted within 14 days of completing a milestone; late invoices will not be paid.
3.4 Each invoice is payable within thirty (30) days of receipt.

4. Intellectual Property
4.1 All rights in the Deliverables, including any pre-existing materials of the Contractor used in them, are assigned to the Client on creation.
4.2 The Contractor may not show the Deliverables in a portfolio without the Client's prior written consent.

5. Confidentiality
5.1 The Contractor must keep the Client's information confidential during this Agreement and for an unlimited period afterwards.

6. Non-Competition
6.1 For twenty-four (24) months after this Agreement ends, the Contractor will not provide services to any business that competes with the Client anywhere in the world.

7. Liability
7.1 The Contractor is liable for all losses suffered by the Client arising from the Services, without limit.
7.2 The Contractor will indemnify the Client against any claim by a third party relating to the Deliverables.

8. Term and Termination
8.1 This Agreement lasts for twelve (12) months and renews automatically for further twelve-month periods unless either party gives notice.
8.2 The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time with immediate effect. The Contractor may terminate on ninety (90) days' written notice.
8.3 On termination the Client will pay only for Deliverables it has accepted.

9. Changes to this Agreement
9.1 The Client may update these terms by posting a new version on its supplier portal. Continued work by the Contractor means acceptance.

10. Disputes
10.1 Any dispute will be resolved by binding arbitration in London. The Contractor waives any right to bring or join a class or collective action.
10.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
