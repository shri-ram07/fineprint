FREELANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT (REVISED DRAFT)

This Agreement is made on 1 October 2026 between Northwind Digital Ltd ("the Client") and Alex Morgan ("the Contractor").

1. Definitions
1.1 "Deliverables" means the work product created by the Contractor specifically for the Client under this Agreement. It does not include materials the Contractor owned before this Agreement.
1.2 "Fees" means GBP 12,000, payable in three equal milestones.

2. Services
2.1 The Contractor will design and build the Client's customer website as described in the project brief.
2.2 Changes to the scope of the Services must be agreed in writing by both parties, including any change to the Fees.

3. Payment
3.1 The Client will pay the Fees within thirty (30) days of receiving a valid invoice.
3.2 Late payments carry interest at 4% per year above the Bank of England base rate.

4. Intellectual Property
4.1 Rights in the Deliverables pass to the Client once the Fees for them have been paid in full.
4.2 The Contractor keeps ownership of pre-existing materials and grants the Client a non-exclusive licence to use them as part of the Deliverables.
4.3 The Contractor may show the Deliverables in a portfolio after the website launches.

5. Confidentiality
5.1 The Contractor must keep the Client's information confidential during this Agreement and for three (3) years afterwards.

6. Non-Solicitation
6.1 For six (6) months after this Agreement ends, the Contractor will not directly solicit the Client's customers that the Contractor worked with.

7. Liability
7.1 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the Fees paid in the twelve months before the claim.

8. Term and Termination
8.1 This Agreement lasts until the Services are completed.
8.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice.
8.3 On termination the Client will pay for all work performed up to the termination date.

9. Disputes
9.1 The parties will try to resolve any dispute by negotiation before starting court proceedings.
9.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
